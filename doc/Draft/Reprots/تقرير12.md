### What I Did Not Prove

- The exact live Worker JavaScript/HTML artifact being served to the user's browser.
- The exact Worker environment variable/key value at runtime.
- Whether a Worker deployment/binding/route is pointing to a stale build, wrong asset, or transformed asset.

### What I Fixed

In this P152 forensic round, no speculative authentication code was changed.
